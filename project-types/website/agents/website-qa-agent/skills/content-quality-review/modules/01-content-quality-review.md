# Skill — Content Quality Review

Evaluate required content completeness and integrity, not copy optimization.

Detect required content missing, placeholder leakage, broken/truncated content and material contradictions.
Do not assume every Customer Profile fact must be public.
Do not flag content merely for being less persuasive, emotional, SEO-oriented or stylistically different.
When Source Design supplies intent but not exact approved wording, reasonable factual wording is allowed.
Avoid duplicate Findings with Requirements, Customer Facts, Localization and Visual Integrity.
