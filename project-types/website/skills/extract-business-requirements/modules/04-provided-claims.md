# 04 — Provided Claims

Separate customer-provided assertions that would require external verification from ordinary operational facts when appropriate.

This includes promotional, comparative, superlative, ranking, market-position, performance, achievement, and quantitative achievement assertions.

A provided claim is not automatically false, suspicious, prohibited, or legally unusable. It means only that the requirements extraction process has not independently verified the assertion.

Preserve the claim's material strength and meaning. Do not strengthen or weaken it.

A single source statement may support both a neutral operational fact and a provided claim when those are genuinely distinct meanings.

Do not classify ordinary operational information as a claim merely because it is customer-provided.

Do not decide legal or commercial publishability here.

Attach only materially supporting source references.
