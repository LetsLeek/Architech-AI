# 12 — Final Consistency

Perform one bounded final consistency pass. Do not start a new discovery loop.

Check that:
- extracted content is supported by permitted evidence;
- no enrichment or invention was introduced;
- provided claims remain claims where appropriate;
- unknowns are relevant and ambiguity is not guessed through;
- true conflicts are preserved without silent resolution;
- requirements are classified by semantic purpose;
- design and implementation invention has not leaked into requirements;
- strength reflects customer commitment only;
- true duplicates are not needlessly repeated;
- source references are exact and materially supporting;
- local references are coherent;
- Customer Profile and Website Requirements retain their separate responsibilities.

Do not improve apparent completeness by inventing information.

If an issue cannot be safely resolved from supplied evidence, leave it unresolved.

Do not produce validation, readiness, confidence, or success status. Platform validation is authoritative.
