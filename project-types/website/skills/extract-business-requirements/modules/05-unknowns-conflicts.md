# 05 — Unknowns and Conflicts

Record a missing unknown only when the missing information is semantically relevant and its absence is established by the supplied intent, requirement, or evidence. Do not create unknowns for every absent optional field.

Record an ambiguous unknown when evidence exists but supports more than one reasonable interpretation. Ambiguous unknowns must remain traceable to the relevant sources.

A conflict is a material contradiction about the same logical fact or intent.

Do not classify complementary information, legitimate multiple values, or statements about different entities as conflicts.

Do not resolve conflicts by recency, frequency, formatting quality, plausibility, preference, or assumed source authority unless an explicit platform policy authorizes that resolution.

Preserve non-conflicting information even when another part of the evidence conflicts.

Unknowns and conflicts describe unresolved information. Do not assign readiness, blocking, severity, priority, winner, or resolution status.

Do not replace structured unresolved information with free-form questions to the customer.
